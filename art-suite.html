Art Suite

A suite inhabited by the universe of Szabolcs Bozó.

At the heart of La Fantaisie, this exclusive junior suite invites you to experience Paris differently: immersed in a decor designed by contemporary artist Szabolcs Bozó, where color, free-form shapes, and imaginary characters transform a hotel room into a living experience.

Conceived as a meeting point between contemporary art and Parisian hospitality, this 42 m² (450 sq ft) junior suite overlooks the hotel's garden, offering a generous, light-filled, and singular space. It features all the comfort of a five-star suite: a king-size bed, a large walk-in closet, a bathroom with both a shower and a bathtub, and a living area infused with the joyful, expressive energy of a fully realized artistic universe.

Based in London, Hungarian artist Szabolcs Bozó is renowned for his colorful creatures, animal figures, and boundless imagination, which is at once spontaneous, childlike, and profoundly contemporary. At La Fantaisie, his visual language finds a natural home: a place where luxury is experienced with joy, curiosity, and a light heart.

Ideal for a Parisian getaway as a couple, a family stay, or an extended escape, the Art Suite accommodates up to two adults and two children. The sofa bed can host a third adult (for an additional fee) or two children under 12 at no extra charge.

A rare, expressive, and joyful suite for those seeking more than just a room: a Fantaisie to be lived.

Exclusive amenities have also been specially created for the occasion, extending this artistic immersion throughout your time at La Fantaisie.

To secure the artistic pieces displayed in the suite, a 1 200€ pre-authorization will be requested upon arrival.
