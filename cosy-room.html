Cosy Room

An intimate hideaway with an enchanting allure.

Dive into our Cosy Room's enchanting allure, drawing inspiration from the artistic atmosphere of Monti with a view on Via Panisperna or the patio.

Thoughtfully designed across 19 to 21 m² (205 to 226 sq ft), the Cosy Room proves that intimacy and comfort go hand in hand. A queen-size bed dressed in satin combed cotton, warm woods, and hand-picked artworks create a refuge that feels less like a hotel room and more like a friend's beautifully kept guest room.

Morning light filters through the tall windows, whether your view opens onto the lively rhythm of Via Panisperna or the quiet green of the patio below. Either way, the neighbourhood's artistic spirit is never far.

Ideal for the solo traveller, the Cosy Room welcomes one guest in perfect comfort — a private retreat for those who travel light and live well.

A warm, intimate room for those seeking more than just a place to sleep: a home to come back to.
