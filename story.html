Every old house
has a story.

them all.

Ralf isn’t the owner.

Not quite a hotel.
Not quite home.

Known.

Not by landmarks.
By favourite tables.
Morning walks.
Quiet corners.

During your stay…

…keep a secret or two.

Some stories are told.
The best ones are lived.
